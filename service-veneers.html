Treatment

Veneers

Porcelain veneers with a shape and shade chosen for your smile.

About the treatment

Veneers are thin porcelain shells bonded to the front of teeth to change their colour, shape or appearance. We assess your teeth and gums first and explain whether veneers suit your concerns.

We discuss the shade, tooth proportions and amount of change you want, then show you the proposed design. Before you agree, we explain how much preparation your teeth may need and what future care involves.

At a glance

  • Changes to tooth colour and shape
  • Shade and proportions chosen with you
  • Porcelain that resists staining, with regular care
  • Preparation explained before treatment
  • A chance to review your proposed smile design

The treatment process

  1. Consultation and assessment. We check your teeth, gums and bite, and discuss the changes you would like to make.
  2. A personal plan. We explain the treatment options, likely results, possible side effects and cost before you decide.
  3. The treatment itself. We talk you through the session and check how you feel as we go.
  4. Aftercare and follow up. We explain how to care for the treated area and when to contact us or return for a review.

Expected results

The change is visible once the veneers are fitted. How long they last depends on your bite, habits and care. We will explain how to protect them and when to return for dental reviews.

Recovery & downtime

Temporary sensitivity can follow preparation and fitting. We will explain cleaning, eating and what to do if your bite feels uncomfortable. Regular dental reviews remain important.

Who is it for?

Veneers may suit adults with healthy teeth and gums who want to address colour, chips, gaps or shape. Grinding, bite problems and the amount of healthy enamel affect suitability. We also discuss alternatives.

Frequently asked questions

Will veneers look fake?
The shade, shape and proportions all affect the look. We discuss your preferences and review the proposed design with you before fitting, aiming for a result that fits your smile.
Do veneers ruin your teeth?
Veneers often require removing some enamel, which cannot grow back. The amount depends on your teeth and the design. We explain this before you decide, along with alternatives and the possibility of future repair or replacement.
How long do they last?
Porcelain veneers may last ten to fifteen years, but this is not guaranteed. Oral hygiene, regular dental reviews and managing tooth grinding all matter. Individual veneers may need repair or replacement sooner.
