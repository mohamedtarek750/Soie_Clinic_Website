Body Contouring

INDIBA radiofrequency sessions for body care, with a plan based on your assessment.

About the treatment

We use INDIBA radiofrequency for body contouring at Soie Clinic. The device delivers energy that warms the treatment area. Before starting, we assess your concerns and explain whether this approach is suitable.

You may be looking to improve firmness, skin texture or the appearance of cellulite. We will discuss what sessions could offer, the limits of treatment and how we will review your progress.

At a glance

  • Treatment without needles or surgery
  • A plan focused on firmness and skin texture
  • Radiofrequency delivered to the selected area
  • Warmth during the session, with settings adjusted to you
  • Advice on care between sessions

The treatment process

  1. Consultation and assessment. We assess the area you want to treat and check whether INDIBA is suitable for you.
  2. A personal plan. We explain the treatment options, likely results, possible side effects and cost before you decide.
  3. The treatment itself. We talk you through the session and check how you feel as we go.
  4. Aftercare and follow up. We explain how to care for the treated area and when to contact us or return for a review.

Expected results

Changes in firmness or texture vary by person and treatment area. We will agree on goals at your consultation and review your response before recommending more sessions.

Recovery & downtime

You may notice temporary warmth or redness after a session. Our team will explain what to expect and whether you need to adjust your routine afterwards.

Who is it for?

It may be considered for concerns about local firmness, skin texture or cellulite. A medical assessment is needed to check suitability, especially if you have implants, take medicines or have had a recent procedure.

Frequently asked questions

Does INDIBA hurt?
You will usually feel warmth in the treated area. Tell the team if it feels too hot or uncomfortable so they can adjust the session.
Is this a treatment for weight loss?
No. Body contouring focuses on a selected area and is not a substitute for weight management. If weight loss is your goal, ask about our nutrition support.
How many sessions will I need?
There is no single schedule for everyone. We will recommend the number and spacing of sessions after assessing the area and your goals.
