Before your visit

INDIBA, explained.

Does Soie Clinic have an INDIBA device?

Yes. We have an INDIBA Deep Care device at Soie Clinic in Cairo. Contact us before visiting to confirm the branch and available appointments.

What is INDIBA Deep Care?

INDIBA Deep Care uses radiofrequency at 448 kHz through the manufacturer's CRET system. Its catalogue describes aesthetic uses for the face and body, as well as support for tissue recovery.

What can INDIBA be used for?

Its catalogue covers face and body aesthetic care and tissue recovery. Our team will assess your concern and explain which uses may be relevant to you.

How many sessions will I need?

That depends on the area, your goals and your response to treatment. We will discuss the number and spacing of sessions after your assessment.

Is INDIBA suitable for everyone?

No. We need to review your medical history first, including pregnancy, implants and electronic devices such as pacemakers. Tell us about medicines and recent procedures too, so we can check whether treatment is suitable.

Before your visit

FineXel, explained.

Does Soie Clinic have a FineXel device?

Yes. The video shows our FineXel fractional CO2 laser at Soie Clinic. Contact us to confirm the branch and arrange a consultation.

What is fractional CO2 laser?

It is a skin resurfacing treatment that applies CO2 laser energy to small areas in a pattern, leaving skin between them untreated. The treatment settings are chosen after a medical assessment.

What can fractional CO2 laser help with?

It may be considered for certain acne scars, uneven texture and fine lines. Suitability depends on the concern and your skin. Results vary, and more than one session may be needed.

How much recovery time will I need?

Recovery depends on the treatment depth, area and your skin. Redness, swelling, peeling and sensitivity can last for several days, with redness sometimes lasting longer. We will discuss timing and aftercare before you book.

Is fractional CO2 laser suitable for everyone?

No. We review your skin tone, healing history, medicines and recent procedures. Tell us about active skin infections, cold sores, pregnancy and recent tanning. We will explain possible risks, including pigment changes and scarring, and whether another approach would suit you.

How many FineXel sessions will I need?

There is no single course for everyone. We suggest a plan after assessing your skin and goals, then review your response before recommending further sessions.
