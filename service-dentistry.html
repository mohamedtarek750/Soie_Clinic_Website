Dentistry

Dental care in Mohandseen, from cleaning and whitening to bonding and smile design.

About the treatment

Our Mohandseen branch offers cleaning, whitening, cosmetic bonding, smile design and porcelain veneers. Tell us what concerns you about your teeth so we can discuss the most suitable options.

We check your teeth and gums first. Any dental health concerns need to be considered before planning cosmetic work.

At a glance

  • Whitening planned after a dental assessment
  • Shade and shape discussed with you
  • Options that consider your natural teeth
  • Dental and aesthetic care within Soie Clinic
  • Cleaning and sterilisation procedures for each visit

The treatment process

  1. Consultation and assessment. We examine your teeth and gums, discuss your concerns and check for underlying problems.
  2. A personal plan. We explain the treatment options, likely results, possible side effects and cost before you decide.
  3. The treatment itself. We talk you through the session and check how you feel as we go.
  4. Aftercare and follow up. We explain how to care for the treated area and when to contact us or return for a review.

Expected results

Results depend on the procedure. Whitening can change tooth colour, while bonding or veneers can change shape and appearance. We will explain the likely result and how to maintain it.

Recovery & downtime

You can usually return to your routine after cleaning, whitening or bonding. Temporary sensitivity may occur, particularly after whitening. More involved work may need several visits, with care instructions at each stage.

Who is it for?

You can book an assessment for concerns about tooth colour, chips, gaps or the appearance of your smile. We will check your oral health and discuss whether cosmetic treatment is appropriate.

Frequently asked questions

Is whitening safe for my teeth?
Whitening needs a dental assessment first. We check your teeth and gums, explain the product and concentration, and discuss possible sensitivity or gum irritation. It does not change the colour of existing fillings or crowns.
What is a Hollywood smile?
It is a term for a plan to improve the colour and shape of the teeth visible when you smile, often using veneers. We discuss the shade, shape and amount of change you want before choosing an approach.
Do you treat dental problems too?
We assess your dental health first and explain which problems need attention before cosmetic treatment. Ask our team about the care available at the Mohandseen branch.
