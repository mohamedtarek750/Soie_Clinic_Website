Medical Facials

Cleansing, exfoliation and hydration chosen for your skin on the day.

About the treatment

A medical facial starts with a skin assessment. Depending on your needs, it may include cleansing, exfoliation, extraction, massage and selected skincare products.

Tell us about sensitivity, breakouts and the products you use at home. We can also discuss options such as Dermapen or OxyGeneo, including whether they suit you and how recovery differs.

At a glance

  • Help with surface buildup and dullness
  • Cleansing and extraction where appropriate
  • Products selected for your skin
  • Separate assessment for Dermapen or OxyGeneo
  • Time to discuss your home routine

The treatment process

  1. Consultation and assessment. We check your skin's condition and sensitivity, and ask what you use at home.
  2. A personal plan. We explain the treatment options, likely results, possible side effects and cost before you decide.
  3. The treatment itself. We talk you through the session and check how you feel as we go.
  4. Aftercare and follow up. We explain how to care for the treated area and when to contact us or return for a review.

Expected results

Skin may feel cleaner, softer and more hydrated after a facial. The visible change and how long it lasts depend on your skin and the steps included.

Recovery & downtime

A simple facial may need little recovery time, though temporary redness can occur. Microneedling and other added procedures can need longer. We will explain aftercare for the session you choose.

Who is it for?

A facial may help with dryness, congestion or dullness, but the steps need to suit your skin. Tell us about active irritation, allergies or recent treatments before booking.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from a spa facial?
At Soie, the session starts with an assessment of your skin and medical history. The products and steps are chosen around that assessment, with advice on aftercare and your home routine.
Can I have a facial before a big event?
Tell us your event date and whether you have had the treatment before. We can suggest timing that allows for possible redness or sensitivity, especially if the session includes extraction or microneedling.
What is OxyGeneo?
OxyGeneo is a facial treatment that combines exfoliation with the application of skincare ingredients. Ask our team which option is available and whether it suits your skin.
