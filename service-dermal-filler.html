Dermal Filler

Hyaluronic acid fillers for volume and contour in areas such as the lips, cheeks and jawline.

About the treatment

Hyaluronic acid fillers are gels placed beneath the skin to add volume or adjust contours. Areas may include the lips, cheeks, chin, jawline, folds beside the mouth and hollows under the eyes. Suitability varies by area and anatomy.

We start by discussing what you would like to change and assessing your facial proportions. We then explain the product, amount and approach we recommend, along with the limits and risks of treatment.

At a glance

  • Volume changes can be seen soon after treatment
  • Product selected for the area being treated
  • Hyaluronic acid filler may be dissolved when medically appropriate
  • Review once swelling has settled
  • A plan based on your facial proportions

The treatment process

  1. Consultation and assessment. We assess the area, review your medical history and ask about any previous filler.
  2. A personal plan. We explain the treatment options, likely results, possible side effects and cost before you decide.
  3. The treatment itself. We talk you through the session and check how you feel as we go.
  4. Aftercare and follow up. We explain how to care for the treated area and when to contact us or return for a review.

Expected results

You may see a change straight away, but swelling can affect the early result. We usually review once it has settled, often around two weeks. Results may last six to eighteen months, depending on the product, area and individual response.

Recovery & downtime

Swelling, tenderness and bruising can last several days, particularly around the lips. We will explain aftercare and the symptoms that need prompt medical attention. Contact us if you have concerns during recovery.

Who is it for?

Fillers may suit adults who want to address volume loss or adjust a facial contour. Your consultation helps us decide whether filler is appropriate and discuss other options when needed.

Frequently asked questions

Will my lips or face look overdone?
Tell us how much change you want. We plan the amount around your features and can take a gradual approach, reviewing the result before considering more treatment.
Is filler safe?
Fillers carry risks, including swelling, bruising and, rarely, blocked blood vessels that can cause serious harm. We discuss these before treatment and explain what to do if symptoms occur. Some hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved, but this does not remove all treatment risks.
What is the difference between Botox and filler?
Botox relaxes muscles that create expression lines. Filler adds volume or changes a contour. We will explain which, if either, is suitable for your concern.
