The experience

From your first visit to aftercare

Here is what usually happens when you come to Soie Clinic for treatment.

1

Consultation

Tell us what you would like help with and ask any questions you have.

2

Skin Assessment

We assess your skin or treatment area and review your medical history.

3

Treatment

We explain each step and check that you are comfortable during the session.

4

Aftercare

You leave with instructions for caring for the treated area at home.

5

Review

We review your progress and discuss whether you need further care.
