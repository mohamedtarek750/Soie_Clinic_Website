Treatment

Botox

Botox can soften expression lines, with the dose and placement chosen for your facial movement.

About the treatment

Botox temporarily relaxes the muscles that cause expression lines, such as frown lines, forehead creases and crow's feet. We assess how your face moves before deciding where to treat and how much to use.

Tell us the change you want and any concerns about your expressions. We will discuss the likely effect and review the result at around two weeks.

At a glance

  • Softens frown lines, forehead creases and crow's feet
  • Treatment planned around your facial movement
  • Treatment usually takes 15 to 20 minutes
  • Dose and placement chosen after assessment
  • Other uses, such as sweating or jaw tension, need a separate assessment

The treatment process

  1. Consultation and assessment. We review your facial movement, medical history and previous injectable treatments.
  2. A personal plan. We explain the treatment options, likely results, possible side effects and cost before you decide.
  3. The treatment itself. We talk you through the session and check how you feel as we go.
  4. Aftercare and follow up. We explain how to care for the treated area and when to contact us or return for a review.

Expected results

Changes usually begin within a few days, with the full effect at around two weeks. Results often last three to five months, depending on the dose, area and your response.

Recovery & downtime

You can usually return to your routine soon after treatment. Small marks, tenderness or bruising may occur around the injection sites. We will explain aftercare, including when to resume exercise and other facial treatments.

Who is it for?

Botox may suit adults looking to soften expression lines. It is not suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or with some neuromuscular conditions. Your consultation includes a review of your medical history and medicines.

Frequently asked questions

Will I look frozen or unnatural?
We aim to soften lines while keeping movement appropriate to your goals. Dose and placement matter, and responses vary. We will discuss the risk of unwanted weakness or asymmetry before treatment and review your result afterwards.
Does it hurt?
Each injection takes a few seconds and may feel like a brief pinch. Tell us if you are sensitive to needles so we can discuss comfort options.
How soon before an event should I book?
Allow at least two to three weeks for the effect to develop and any bruising to settle. If it is your first treatment, tell us the event date so we can advise on timing.
