Before your visit

Cutera, explained.

Does Soie Clinic have a Cutera device?

Yes. We have a Cutera excel HR device at Soie Clinic in Cairo. Contact us before visiting to confirm the branch and available appointments.

Which Cutera device is featured here?

This is Cutera excel HR, with 755 nm and 1064 nm wavelength options. Both clips were filmed at Soie Clinic.

How many Cutera sessions will I need?

It depends on your skin, hair, treatment area and goals. We will suggest a schedule after your assessment and review how you respond.

How do I book a Cutera consultation?

Message our team with the treatment or concern you would like to discuss. We will confirm the branch and help you arrange a consultation.
