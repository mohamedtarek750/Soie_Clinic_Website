Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Here are answers to common questions about visiting Soie Clinic. If you need more information, message us on WhatsApp.

Do I need a consultation before booking a treatment?
Yes, for injectables and other treatments that need a medical assessment. We review your medical history, discuss your goals and explain the options first. You can usually book a facial directly, with a skin assessment before it begins.
What standards guide care at Soie Clinic?
Our medical team assesses each patient before treatment and follows the clinic's hygiene and preparation protocols. We explain the products or devices involved, possible side effects and the care you will need afterwards.
Will my results look natural?
We plan treatment with your existing features in mind and discuss how much change you want. Results depend on the treatment and your individual response, so we agree on realistic expectations before starting.
How do I book an appointment?
Choose your branch, treatment, date and time on the booking page, then add your contact details. Reception will contact you to confirm availability. You can also book by messaging either branch on WhatsApp.
Where are you located and what are your hours?
We have two branches: UMC 216 in New Cairo and 41st Lebanon Street in Mohandseen. Both are open seven days a week: 10 AM to 11 PM Saturday through Thursday, and 12 PM to 10 PM on Friday.
Do treatments hurt?
It depends on the treatment and your sensitivity. We can discuss options such as numbing cream or cooling where appropriate. Tell the team if you are worried about discomfort, and let us know how you feel during the session.
How much do treatments cost?
The price depends on the treatment, area and products needed. Message us for current prices. We will confirm the cost of your plan before treatment.
Is there any downtime after treatments?
Recovery depends on the treatment and your skin. Some facials allow a quick return to your routine, while injections, peels and other procedures may cause redness, swelling or bruising. We will explain the expected recovery before you book.
Can I see examples of your results?
Yes. The Before & After page shows photos shared with our clients' consent. You can also ask about examples relevant to your concerns during your consultation.
Do you treat both women and men?
Yes. We welcome women and men and plan treatment according to each person's needs and medical assessment.

Have another question? Ask us.

Message either branch on WhatsApp. Our team will help with treatment enquiries, prices and appointments.
